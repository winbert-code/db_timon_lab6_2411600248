Gradebook notes

GRADES is a ternary relationship with the composite primary key (student_id, course_id, type_id): one grade per student, per course, per assignment type. It has three foreign keys, to students, courses and assignment_types. assignment_types.default_weight (CHECK 0..100) drives the weighted final grade in sp_CalculateCourseGrade.

Views

  • student_grade_summary — grades joined to students, courses and types (Student dashboard).
  • teacher_course_overview — per-course/type submissions and average grades, with user_id for teacher filtering.
  • student_transcript — enrollment-based transcript with weight, grade and instructor.
  • course_performance_analytics — enrolled students, grades submitted, average/minimum/maximum grade per course.
